# Support – AI Security Audit Checklist & AI Risk Scanner

Welcome to the support page for the **AI Security Audit Checklist** repository and the **AI Risk Scanner mobile application**, developed by SecurityOrb.

This page provides guidance on usage, troubleshooting, reporting issues, and getting assistance.

---

## 📌 Overview

The AI Risk Scanner and associated checklist framework are designed to help users:

- Assess AI system risk quickly  
- Apply governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) principles  
- Generate structured, defensible outputs  
- Support decision-making aligned with NIST AI RMF
