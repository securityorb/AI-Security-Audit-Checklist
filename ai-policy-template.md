# AI Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose
To ensure safe and responsible use of AI systems.

---

## Scope
Applies to all employees and systems using AI.

---

## Requirements

### Data Protection
- No sensitive data without approval
- Encryption required

### Access Control
- Role-based access enforced

### Human Oversight
- High-risk outputs must be reviewed

### Monitoring
- Continuous monitoring required

---

## Compliance
Aligned with NIST AI RMF principles.

---

## Enforcement
Violations may result in disciplinary action.
